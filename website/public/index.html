Why now

Talking to a machine is moving from turns to time.

Chat is a transcript with a delay: you type, you wait, it answers, you wait. Aggie runs on a clock instead. It listens, watches your screen, and acts while you keep working. Once it feels like a person in the room, you do not go back.
